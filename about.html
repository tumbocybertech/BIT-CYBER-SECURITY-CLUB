About us

Club history

BIT Cyber Shield Club began when a small group of BIT students wanted a place to learn security properly and share what they learned. It has grown into a college-wide community of learners, builders and mentors.

Mission

Provide practical, ethical cybersecurity education and a supportive community for every BIT student.

Vision

Be East Africa's most respected student cybersecurity community.

Core values

Integrity

We act honestly and only with permission.

Curiosity

We ask how things work and why they fail.

Collaboration

We share knowledge and lift each other up.

Responsibility

We protect people, data and privacy.

Club objectives

  • Teach cybersecurity fundamentals to students of every course.
  • Prepare members for CTFs, certifications and careers.
  • Run awareness campaigns for the wider BIT community.
  • Support student research and innovation projects.
  • Build partnerships with industry and other institutions.

Leadership

The club is run by an elected executive team and department leaders, guided by a faculty patron. Meet the team.
